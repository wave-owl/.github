# Wave Owl

**Amateur-radio software for exploring more of the spectrum.**

Wave Owl is an independent amateur-radio software effort focused on practical tools for receiving, decoding and exploring real on-air signals.

## A wideband, multi-mode skimmer

The central goal is a receive-first skimmer built primarily around the **Hermes Lite 2 SDR**: monitor multiple signals across a wide slice of spectrum, decode CW and a broad range of digital modes in parallel, and make band activity easier to see and explore. Think less *one frequency, one decoder* and more *what is happening across the band right now?*

This is an ongoing effort. Broad mode support, useful live views and practical spotting are goals—not a claim that every mode or workflow is finished.

## Built for radio people

We care about real receivers, real signals and tools that are useful at the station—whether you are chasing CW, experimenting with digital modes, or curious about what is active on the bands. AI-assisted development is one of our tools; making useful radio software is the point.

Ideas, bug reports and contributions are welcome. Explore Wave Owl's work through the organization profile and its repositories.
